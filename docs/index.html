How it feels

Less app. More momentum.

Chatter is designed to disappear while you are working, then give you just enough feedback to know what is happening.

1

Hold to talk

Press your chosen key anywhere on your Mac. The notch responds immediately with sound and motion.

2

Watch your words arrive

The live transcript follows your newest words while the mascot keeps the moment friendly.

3

Release and keep going

Chatter finishes, optionally tidies the text, and places it into the app that already has your focus.

Local by design

Simple on the surface. Capable underneath.

The technical choices are there to support the experience, not become the experience.

01 · Live dictation

One model keeps the first word fast.

Nemotron 3.5 streaming handles the live path. There is no second speech-recognition model waiting behind it.

02 · Your writing style

Optional local cleanup when you want it.

A small local language model can add punctuation, repair boundaries, and format clear lists without sending your words away.

03 · Personal vocabulary

Chatter remembers corrections you teach it.

Your dictionary keeps names, jargon, and phrases that matter to you close at hand.

04 · A calmer interface

Useful details are there when you need them.

History, insights, files, and advanced model controls are available without crowding the moment you are trying to write.
