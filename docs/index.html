The everyday flow

Three small moments. No app switching.

Chatter is built to get out of the way. The feedback is there when you need it and quiet when you do not.

01

Press and speak

Hold your chosen key anywhere on the Mac. The sound and notch HUD respond immediately.

02

See the words arrive

The live preview follows your newest words while the focused app stays right where you left it.

03

Release and continue

Chatter finishes the transcript, applies optional cleanup, and pastes it into the active app.

Two jobs, clearly separated

Fast when you are talking. Careful when you are exporting.

Live dictation and file transcription have different needs, so Chatter gives each one the model slot and controls it deserves.

Live dictation

Keep your first words moving.

Nemotron 3.5 streaming handles the live path from audio capture through the raw transcript. The optional cleanup model is not a second speech recognizer.

Transcribed files

Bring the longer things home.

Drop in audio or video, choose the timing format you need, and export plain text or subtitles. Parakeet is a good starting point for word-level timing.

Personal vocabulary

Teach it the words you use.

Keep names, jargon, and corrected word boundaries in a local dictionary. It is personalization you can see and edit.

A calmer Mac app

Useful details, in their place.

History, insights, model choices, and advanced settings are available without crowding the moment you are trying to write.

Start with the sensible setup

Your Mac does not need a model catalog.

Choose the model for the job, then let Chatter keep the rest out of your way. The short guide explains the trade-offs by unified memory and links to the downloads.

Read the three-step model guide
8 GB

Keep it lean

Use Nemotron 3.5 for live dictation. Leave cleanup off until the raw path feels right.

16 GB

Good starting point

Nemotron plus a small 2B-4B cleanup model gives you room for punctuation and formatting.

24 GB+

Experiment carefully

Try a larger cleanup model if you want, but keep an eye on the latency of your own workflow.

Models are downloaded separately. Chatter does not bundle several gigabytes into the app.

The promise

Your voice stays yours.

Chatter is designed around local processing. Your microphone audio, transcripts, cleanup prompts, dictionary, and usage history stay on this Mac. There is no account to create.
